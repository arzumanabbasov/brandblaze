# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

BrandBlaze contributors are expected to create a respectful, constructive, and
welcoming environment. Participate with empathy, assume good intent, give
actionable feedback, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate
intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be
reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as practical.
